# 10-Minute Pre-Call Audit Checklist

Saved 2026-11-23, pasted by Demetrius.
Fast pre-call audit to give sharp, specific context before a discovery call with a prospect — especially an SBA owner-operator — without getting bogged down in over-analysis.

Note: the pasted note as received lists the question-conversion section and the rule of thumb below; the item-by-item checklist itself was not in the paste. Add it here when Demetrius sends it, rather than inventing items.

## How to turn audit notes into sharp call questions

- **From SBA Context:** "Since you stepped into `[Company Name]` after the transition, how much of the current intake and follow-up process came from the previous setup versus something you built yourself?"
- **From Website & Intake:** "When I pulled up `[Domain.com]` on my phone, I noticed inquiries go through `[form / email / call]` — what happens on your end when a request comes in while you're tied up?"
- **From Google Reviews:** "You've built up `[X]` reviews with a `[rating]` average in `[City]`, so demand clearly isn't the issue — where do leads usually stall between first checking you out and actually getting booked?"

## Quick rule of thumb before you dial

Keep audit notes to **three bullet points max** on one screen:
1. **What seems to be working**
2. **Where leads or hours likely leak**
3. **The single question that tests that leak first**

Over-prepping a 20-point checklist often makes you talk too much instead of diagnosing what the owner actually cares about.

## Use in the call flow

Do this audit in the 10 minutes before the 15-min discovery call, using the enriched prospect row (Google rating/review count, website, friction tags) and the SBA context (acquisition date, loan, NAICS).
