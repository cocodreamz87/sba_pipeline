# Deposit / Invoice Gate Reminder

Saved 2026-11-23, pasted by Demetrius.
Send right after a client approves a change order or SOW — keeps build work cleanly gated until the deposit clears, without sounding pushy.

## Email Version

**Subject:** Quick reminder: `[Company Name]` deposit invoice to lock in your build slot

> Hi `[First Name]`,
> Thanks again for signing off on `[project / change-order title]`.
> Just a quick reminder that the deposit invoice (`$[Amount]`) is ready here: `[Payment / Invoice Link]`. As soon as that clears, I'll lock in your build slot and kick off work right away (`[estimated turnaround, e.g., 3–5 business days from clearance]`).
> Let me know if you need the invoice resent or routed to anyone else on your team.
> Best,
> `[Your Name]`

## Slack / Quick-Note Version

> Hey `[First Name]` — thanks for approving `[change-order / project title]`! Quick reminder on the deposit invoice (`$[Amount]`): `[Payment Link]`. Once that clears, I'll lock in the build slot and start right away.

## Why This Keeps Boundaries Effortless

- **Ties payment directly to start date:** Framing payment clearance as the trigger that locks in their turnaround clock makes the gate feel operational and fair rather than confrontational.
- **Offers an easy out for accounting delays:** Asking whether the invoice needs to go to someone else on their team gives busy owners a friction-free way to forward it immediately.
