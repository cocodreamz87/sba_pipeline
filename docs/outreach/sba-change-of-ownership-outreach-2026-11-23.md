# SBA 7(a) Change-of-Ownership Outreach — Post-Acquisition Modernization

Saved 2026-11-23, pasted by Demetrius.
Track: ACQUISITION_NEW_OPERATOR (from `sba_prospects` campaign_track).

Psychology: distinct from general cold outreach. They just put personal skin in the game, took on bank debt, and inherited a business whose previous owner often ran operations on legacy software, spreadsheets, paper forms, or a neglected website. Fatigued by "AI guru" hype and wary of massive software retainers right after closing. Sharpest angle: **post-acquisition modernization without disrupting cash flow** — modernize the web front-end so new customers convert effortlessly while wiring deterministic automations behind the scenes to eliminate manual handoffs.

## 1. Cold Email Sequence (2-Step Post-Acquisition Angle)

### Email 1 — Initial Outreach (Signal + Inherited Friction)

- **Subject:** `[Company Name]` after the transition / web + intake
- **Body:**
> Hi `[First Name]`,
> Congrats on taking over `[Company Name]` — buying an established shop with real history and `[X]+ Google reviews` in `[City]` is a huge move, though inheriting the previous owner's tech and day-to-day handoffs usually comes with a few surprises.
> A common bottleneck we see right after an SBA acquisition: the business already has steady demand, but the website still routes inquiries through a slow form or inbox while someone manually keys details into scheduling, quoting, or your CRM.
> We help new owners modernize their web presence and automate repetitive intake/follow-up workflows — without ripping out what already works — so leads get booked faster and fewer tasks sit on your desk at night.
> Open to a quick 90-second video showing two easy wins I spotted on `[Domain.com]`?
> Best,
> `[Your Name]`

### Email 2 — Follow-Up (Low-Friction Teardown Offer)

- **Subject:** Re: `[Company Name]` after the transition / web + intake
- **Body:**
> Hi `[First Name]`,
> Figured you're knee-deep in operations right now, so I kept this brief.
> When we audited `[Domain.com]`, two things stood out for a new owner trying to capture more of that local demand without adding admin headcount:
> 1. `[Specific gap, e.g., Mobile visitors currently hit a basic contact form with no instant booking or automated confirmation]`
> 2. `[Specific gap, e.g., Inquiries and quote requests appear to rely on manual email/phone follow-up rather than instant routing]`
>
> Happy to record a 90-second Loom walking through how a cleaner web intake + automated follow-up setup would look for `[Company Name]`. Want me to send that over?
> Best,
> `[Your Name]`

## 2. 20-Second Cold Call & Voicemail Script

### Live Call Opener (~20 Seconds)

> "Hey `[First Name]`, this is `[Your Name]` — I'll keep this to 20 seconds. I noticed you recently stepped into `[Company Name]` in `[City]`, and I sent a quick email about a couple of easy web and intake automation wins I spotted on `[Domain.com]` so leads don't slip through while you're running the transition. Have you had a chance to look at how inquiries come in right now, or should I just send over a 90-second video?"

### Voicemail Script (~20 Seconds)

> "Hey `[First Name]`, `[Your Name]` calling. Saw you took over `[Company Name]` recently — congrats on the move. I sent a quick note to your email after spotting a simple way to tighten up web intake and automate follow-up on `[Domain.com]` without adding busywork for your team. I'll shoot over a 90-second video breakdown too — no rush, just wanted to put a name to the note."

## 3. Execution Tips for Change-of-Ownership Prospects

- **Anchor to Continuity, Not "Blowing Everything Up":** New owners protect existing customer relationships and proven workflows. Position automations as *guardrails and time-savers* around their current process rather than a risky overhaul.
- **Keep the First Ask Tiny:** Ask permission to send a **90-second video** or **2-point teardown**, not a 45-minute strategy call on touch one.
- **Pair Call + Email Within 24–48 Hours:** Calling 1–2 days after Email 1 gives you a natural, non-intrusive reason to reference a specific note already sitting in their inbox.
