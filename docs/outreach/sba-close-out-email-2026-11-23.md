# Close-Out Email (After Two Follow-Ups)

Saved 2026-11-23, pasted by Demetrius.
Short, respectful close-out for a non-responsive prospect — a clean exit without guilt or pressure, which often prompts a reply when timing or bandwidth was simply holding them back. Send after the recap + 3-day nudge + 4–5 more days of silence.

## Template

**Subject:** Re: `[Company Name]` recap: web intake + automation options

> Hi `[First Name]`,
> Since I haven't heard back, I'm going to close out your project folder for now so I'm not cluttering your inbox while you focus on `[Company Name]`.
> No hard feelings at all — timing, budget, or post-transition priorities can shift fast. If tightening up web intake or automating routine follow-up moves back up your list later this quarter or next, feel free to pick this thread right back up.
> Wishing you and the team a smooth run ahead.
> Best,
> `[Your Name]`

## Variations

### Low-lift opening (down-sell / later timing)

> Hi `[First Name]`,
> I'll close this out for now so I'm not adding noise to a packed schedule. If **Tier 2** isn't the right fit right now — or if things settle down in a month or two — just reply whenever the timing makes sense and we can revisit where `[Company Name]` stands then.
> All the best,
> `[Your Name]`

### Strong bottlenecks on the discovery call (high-signal soft exit)

> Hi `[First Name]`,
> Going to pause outreach on this for now since I haven't connected with you — I know `[mention bottleneck, e.g., daily ops / quote follow-up]` can eat up whole days fast. Whenever you're ready to take that off your plate, this thread will be here.
> Best,
> `[Your Name]`

## Why this works

- **Zero guilt or passive-aggressiveness:** Avoiding lines like *"I guess you aren't interested"* or *"Per my last email"* keeps your reputation clean — especially valuable in tight-knit local markets or SBA borrower/lender circles.
- **Clean pipeline hygiene:** Marking the lead as closed/paused in Supabase or your CRM stops endless chasing and frees your attention for active conversations and fresh prospects.
