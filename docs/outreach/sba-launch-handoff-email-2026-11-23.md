# Tier 2 Launch & Handoff Email

Saved 2026-11-23, pasted by Demetrius.
Confirm production go-live, summarize what's live, set clear recurring care expectations, and establish clean support boundaries from Day 1.

## Template

**Subject:** `[Company Name]` Tier 2 is live — launch summary, care plan & support guide

> Hi `[First Name]`,
> `[Company Name]` is officially live in production—here is your quick launch recap and handoff guide:
> **1. What Is Now Live**
> * **Production Site & Intake:** `[Live URL]` (mobile layout, forms/booking flow, SSL, and DNS cutover verified)
> * **Active Automation Workflows:**
> 1. **Speed-to-Lead & Qualification Routing**
> 2. **Booking & Appointment Reminders**
> 3. **Post-Completion Review / Follow-Up Loop**
>
> **2. Recurring Care (`$650/mo`)**
> * **Included:** Ongoing uptime/workflow monitoring, routine maintenance, minor copy/link adjustments, and standard optimization tweaks as real traffic rolls in.
> * **Next Check-In:** Let's do a quick **14-day post-launch check-in** on `[Day, Date]` to review early lead/booking flow and any real-world edge cases.
>
> **3. Support & Scope Boundaries**
> * **Included Support:** Fixes for broken site/workflow behavior, monitoring alerts, and minor adjustments covered under monthly care.
> * **New Scope / Change Orders:** Brand-new pages, custom third-party integrations, major workflow rebuilds, or net-new campaign builds outside the original Tier 2 SOW are scoped as separate add-ons/change orders before work starts.
> * **Where to Send Issues:** Reply here or post in `[shared Slack channel / support email]` with the page URL or workflow name and what happened.
>
> Congrats on getting this launched—excited to see real leads and bookings run through a much cleaner system.
> Best,
> `[Your Name]`

## Why This Handoff Works Cleanly

- **Sets Day-1 guardrails without friction:** Putting recurring care and out-of-scope rules in writing right at launch prevents "quick favors" from quietly eroding your post-launch margin.
- **Gives an immediate early-feedback checkpoint:** Scheduling a 14-day check-in gives the client a defined moment to bring real-world observations after actual leads and bookings hit the system.
