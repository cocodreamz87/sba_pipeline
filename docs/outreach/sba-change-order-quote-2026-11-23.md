# Change-Order Quote Template

Saved 2026-11-23, pasted by Demetrius.
Fixed price, exact deliverables, estimated turnaround — client approves cleanly in a single reply. Build work stays gated until sign-off.

## Email Version

**Subject:** Change order quote: `[Company Name]` — `[short title of requested addition]`

> Hi `[First Name]`,
> Here is the fixed quote for adding `[brief description of requested addition]` to `[Company Name]`:
> * **Fixed Price:** `$[Amount]` (`[e.g., 50% upfront / 50% on delivery — or due on approval]`)
> * **Included Deliverables:**
> * `[Deliverable 1 — e.g., Build and configure the 4th workflow trigger, routing rules, and Slack/SMS alerts]`
> * `[Deliverable 2 — e.g., Test end-to-end with sample submissions and deploy to production]`
>
> * **Estimated Turnaround:** `[e.g., 3–5 business days from approval, payment/deposit clearance, and any needed access/assets]`
> * **Out of Scope:** `[1 line noting what isn't included—e.g., ongoing third-party usage/carrier fees or extra revisions beyond one polish pass]`
>
> Just reply **"Approved"** here if you'd like me to queue this up and get started.
> Best,
> `[Your Name]`

## Slack / Quick-Note Version

> Hey `[First Name]` — quick fixed quote for `[requested addition]`:
> * **Price:** `$[Amount]` (`[payment terms]`)
> * **Deliverables:** `[1–2 concrete items]`
> * **Turnaround:** `[e.g., 3–4 business days]` once approved
>
> Reply **"Approved"** if you want me to kick it off.

## Why This Format Works

- **Removes ambiguity before build:** Listing exact deliverables and a one-line out-of-scope note prevents moving goalposts mid-task.
- **Single-word approval friction:** Giving a clear **"Approved"** reply prompt makes saying yes effortless while keeping a clear paper trail in email or Slack.
