## How to use this right after the call

1. **Capture verbatim language first:** Writing down one or two exact phrases the owner used makes your recap email sound genuinely attentive rather than templated.
2. **Lock the math before momentum fades:** Plugging in their numbers immediately ensures your recap email quotes the exact payback estimate you walked through together.
3. **Update your status & follow-up timer:** Log the chosen tier and set a 3-day reminder to send your nudge if they haven't replied to the recap.
