# 3-Day Follow-Up Nudge

Saved 2026-11-23, pasted by Demetrius.
Short, polite nudge to get a clear signal from a busy owner / SBA operator without sounding pushy, needy, or repetitive. Send 3 days after the post-discovery recap email, in the same thread.

## Template

**Subject:** Re: `[Company Name]` recap: web intake + automation options

> Hi `[First Name]`,
> Quick bump on this in case it got buried between calls and day-to-day fires.
> Did **Tier `[2 or 1]`** feel like the right direction for `[Company Name]` right now, or did priorities shift on your end? Happy to adjust the scope or close this out cleanly either way — just let me know what makes the most sense.
> Best,
> `[Your Name]`

## Variations

### If they seemed excited on the call (Momentum Nudge)

> Hi `[First Name]`,
> Just floating this to the top of your inbox so `[Company Name]` doesn't lose momentum while things are busy. Ready to reserve a build slot for **Tier `[2 or 1]`** whenever you are — want me to send over the kickoff details or jump on a quick 10-minute wrap-up?
> Best,
> `[Your Name]`

### If budget or timing was a question mark (Low-Pressure Pivot)

> Hi `[First Name]`,
> Totally get how fast the week moves once operations pick up. If **Tier 2** feels heavy right now, **Tier 1 ($3,500)** is a straightforward way to fix `[their #1 bottleneck, e.g., slow lead follow-up]` first and roll that into a bigger build later.
> Worth a quick 10-minute chat this week, or should I check back next month?
> Best,
> `[Your Name]`

## Timing & cadence

- Reply directly inside the **same email thread** as the discovery call recap, so all three tiers and bottleneck notes stay visible in one place.
- If they still don't reply after 4–5 more days, send one calm closing note leaving the door open rather than chasing indefinitely.
