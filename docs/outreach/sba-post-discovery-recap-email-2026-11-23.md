# Post-Discovery Call Recap Email

Saved 2026-11-23, pasted by Demetrius.
Send within 2 hours of the 15-min discovery call. Mirrors their exact bottlenecks back, connects the 3 productized tiers to those leaks, ends with a low-friction binary choice.

## Template

**Subject:** `[Company Name]` recap: web intake + automation options

> Hi `[First Name]`,
> Great speaking with you earlier — appreciate you making time between `[mention specific context from call, e.g., site visits / ops / client calls]`.
> **What We Flagged Today**
> * **Front-end friction:** `[1-line summary, e.g., Mobile visitors hitting a slow form or static inquiry flow without instant booking]`
> * **Manual follow-up / handoff lag:** `[1-line summary, e.g., Leads or quote requests waiting on manual inbox checks, callbacks, or re-entry]`
> * **Cost to the business:** `[1-line summary, e.g., ~X hours/week of manual coordination and warm leads cooling off before response]`
>
> **How We Can Solve It (3 Options)**
> 1. **Tier 1 — Conversion & Intake Sprint ($3,500 one-time | ~10 business days):** High-converting landing/intake funnel + **1 core automation** (instant SMS/email confirmation, calendar/CRM routing, and real-time alert). Best if you want to plug lead leakage fast without a full site rebuild.
> 2. **Tier 2 — Full Revenue & Ops Engine ($7,500 setup + $650/mo | ~3–4 weeks):** Full custom site (up to 7 pages) + **up to 3 deterministic workflows** (smart lead routing, booking/no-show reminders, and post-job review requests), plus ongoing monitoring & care. Best fit for modernizing your web presence and automating routine back-office handoffs end-to-end.
> 3. **Tier 3 — Custom Operations & Portal Suite ($14,500+ setup + $1,250/mo | ~6–8 weeks):** Custom web platform + client/customer portal + multi-step quoting, billing/CRM sync, and custom logic workflows.
>
> **Next Step**
> Based on what you shared, **Tier `[2 or 1]`** is the cleanest match for `[Company Name]` right now.
> Want to lock in a build slot and kick off with the initial deposit, or hop on a quick 10-minute wrap-up on `[Day, e.g., Thursday at 9:30 AM or 4:30 PM]` to finalize which tier makes the most sense? Just reply **1, 2, 3, or "call"** and I'll handle the rest.
> Best,
> `[Your Name]`

## Tips

- **Send within 2 hours of the call:** momentum with busy owners drops sharply after 24 hours once they get pulled back into daily fires.
- **Keep bullet points punchy:** one crisp sentence per bottleneck proves you listened without forcing them to read a wall of text on their phone.
- **Give an easy reply keyword:** reply `1`, `2`, `3`, or `"call"` — removes friction vs. hunting for a calendar link or writing a long response.
