# Staging Build & Workflow Demo Email

Saved 2026-11-23, pasted by Demetrius.
Share a Tier 2 (Full Revenue & Ops Engine) staging site and workflow walkthrough with a client, guide their review, and collect consolidated feedback on schedule.

## Template

**Subject:** `[Company Name]` Tier 2 staging build & workflow walkthrough ready for review

> Hi `[First Name]`,
> Your **Tier 2** staging build and workflow walkthrough are ready to review:
> * **Staging Site Preview:** `[Staging URL]`
> * **Workflow Walkthrough (`[e.g., 5–7 min Loom / Recorded Demo]`):** `[Walkthrough Link]`
>
> **What to Focus On During Your Review**
> 1. **Pages & Mobile Flow:** Check layout, copy, service/booking clarity, and mobile experience across the pages we built.
> 2. **3 Automation Workflows:** Review the walkthrough showing how new inquiries route, how appointment reminders fire, and how post-completion follow-ups/reviews trigger.
>
> **How to Share Feedback**
> * Please gather any notes from your team into **one consolidated list** and reply by **`[Day, Date — within 5 business days]`** so we can knock out your revision round in a single pass.
> * Once final polish is wrapped and **Milestone 2 (`$3,000`)** is settled, we'll lock in production launch and DNS cutover.
>
> Let me know if anything feels off or if you'd like to hop on a quick 10-minute review call.
> Best,
> `[Your Name]`

## Tip

- **Keep feedback anchored to URLs + workflow names:** Ask clients to group notes by page URL and workflow name (Workflow 1 / 2 / 3) — prevents scattered one-off messages and keeps revision rounds clean and deterministic.
