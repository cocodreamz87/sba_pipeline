# Out-of-Scope Change-Order Message

Saved 2026-11-23, pasted by Demetrius.
Send via email or Slack when a client asks for a post-launch addition outside the agreed Tier 2 SOW — neutral, helpful, clear on next steps.

## Email Version

**Subject:** Re: `[Company Name]` — `[requested feature/workflow/page]` scope & quick quote

> Hi `[First Name]`,
> Happy to take a look at `[brief description of requested addition—e.g., adding a 4th automation workflow / building a new landing page / connecting a new tool]`.
> That falls outside the original **Tier 2** launch scope, so it would be handled as a quick change order rather than routine monthly care (`$650/mo`). If you'd like me to build it, I can send over a fixed quote and timeline once I confirm the exact logic/requirements with you.
> Want me to put together that quick quote for you?
> Best,
> `[Your Name]`

## Slack / Quick-Note Version

> Hey `[First Name]` — `[brief description of request]` is outside the original **Tier 2** scope, so I can price that out as a quick change order for you. Want me to send over a quick quote and timeline before we build it?

## Why This Works

- **Zero defensiveness:** Framing it as a standard boundary rather than a "no" keeps the working relationship smooth while protecting your build and care margin.
- **Clear gate before work starts:** Asking whether they want the quote first stops accidental unpaid build work before approval.
