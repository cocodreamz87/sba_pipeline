# Post-Kickoff Recap Email

Saved 2026-11-23, pasted by Demetrius.
Lock in everything decided on the Tier 2 kickoff call, confirm target dates, and prompt quick completion of any remaining onboarding access or assets so you can start building immediately. Send within 1–2 hours of the kickoff call.

## Template

**Subject:** `[Company Name]` kickoff recap, timeline & next steps

> Hi `[First Name]`,
> Great kicking things off with you today. Below is a quick recap of what we locked in for **Tier 2 (Full Revenue & Ops Engine)** so we have a clean shared reference point:
> **1. Agreed Sitemap (Up to 7 Pages)**
> * `[e.g., Home, Services, About/Trust, Reviews/Proof, Quote/Booking, Contact, Legal/Privacy]`
> * **Primary conversion focus:** `[e.g., Mobile-first quote/booking flow so leads can schedule or request an estimate without back-and-forth]`
>
> **2. Agreed Automation Workflows (Up to 3)**
> 1. **Speed-to-Lead & Qualification Routing:** `[1-line summary of trigger, instant confirmation, and alert destination]`
> 2. **Booking & Appointment Reminders:** `[1-line summary of calendar sync, reminder timing, and reschedule handling]`
> 3. **Post-Completion Review / Follow-Up Loop:** `[1-line summary of trigger milestone, timing, and exception routing]`
>
> **3. Target Dates**
> * **Assets & Access Due:** `[Day, Date — e.g., within 3 business days]`
> * **Staging & Workflow Walkthrough:** `[Day, Date — Week 3]`
> * **Target Go-Live:** `[Day, Date — Week 4]`
>
> **4. Still Needed From Your Side to Start Build**
> * `[Bullet list of remaining logins, DNS/hosting access, brand files, SMS/A2P details, or workflow answers — or write "Everything received — moving straight into build."]`
>
> Drop any remaining items in `[shared channel / onboarding link]` whenever you have a few minutes, and reply here if anything above needs adjusting.
> Best,
> `[Your Name]`

## Tip

- **Keep the "Still Needed" list actionable:** Only list what is actually missing — nothing slows down a new client faster than making them wade through items they already handed over on the call.
