# SBA Productized Pricing — 3 Tiers

Saved 2026-11-23, pasted by Demetrius.
Anchored on **speed-to-lead, conversion, and hours saved** — not page counts or hourly billing. Low-risk entry (Tier 1), "most popular" full-system build (Tier 2, core offer), custom anchor (Tier 3) that makes Tier 2 feel like the obvious high-ROI decision.

**Note on existing pricing:** this is far above the canonical Coco Club Live cheat sheet ($299 one-page / $750 5-page / $250 booking / $950 Launch). This is a separate, SBA owner-operator productized structure. Do not overwrite the canonical sheet with these numbers until Demetrius says so.

## Tier 1: Conversion & Intake Sprint — $3,500 (One-Time)

- **Core Promise:** Stop losing warm mobile traffic and cut lead response time from hours to under 60 seconds — without rebuilding your entire website.
- **What's Included:**
  - **Frontend:** Conversion-engineered primary landing page, mobile-first service/offer layout, friction-free embedded booking/quote flow (deployed on a fast subdomain or integrated cleanly into their existing domain).
  - **Automation (1 Workflow):** Deterministic webhook pipeline connecting form/calendar submissions directly to instant SMS + email confirmation for the prospect, automated CRM/Airtable lead creation, and real-time Slack or SMS notification to the owner.
  - **Handoff:** Full documentation, 30-minute Loom training walkthrough, and 14 days of post-launch bug coverage.
- **Explicit Out-of-Scope Guardrails:** Full multi-page copywriting, custom brand identity/logo design, or legacy database migrations.

## Tier 2: Full Revenue & Ops Engine — $7,500 Setup + $650/mo *(Recommended Anchor)*

- **Core Promise:** Replace an outdated, static brochure site with a modern web presence wired directly into automated scheduling, follow-up, and reputation workflows.
- **What's Included:**
  - **Frontend:** Custom UI/UX design and responsive build (up to 7 pages: Home, Services, About/Trust, Case Studies/Reviews, Quote/Booking Flow, Contact, Legal), technical SEO foundation, and sub-second mobile load optimization.
  - **Automation (Up to 3 Deterministic Pipelines):**
    1. **Speed-to-Lead & Qualification:** Smart multi-step intake form that routes high-value inquiries immediately while filtering spam/out-of-area requests.
    2. **Booking & No-Show Recovery:** Calendar sync with automated 24-hour/1-hour SMS & email reminders plus self-serve rescheduling links.
    3. **Post-Service Review Engine:** Automated trigger upon job/appointment completion requesting a 5-star Google review (routing unhappy feedback privately to the owner first).
  - **Ongoing Care & Monitoring ($650/mo — 3-month minimum, then month-to-month):** Managed cloud hosting, uptime/SSL monitoring, webhook failure alerts, API token maintenance, and up to 2 hours/month of minor copy or workflow adjustments.

## Tier 3: Custom Operations & Portal Suite — Starting at $14,500 + $1,250/mo

- **Core Promise:** Eliminate manual admin bottlenecks across quoting, client onboarding, scheduling, and billing with a custom web application and deep system integrations.
- **What's Included:**
  - **Frontend & Client Portal:** Custom public website plus a secure customer/client portal (magic-link login, project/job status tracking, document uploads, or self-serve invoice payment).
  - **Automation & Logic Engine:** Custom rule-based quoting/pricing calculators, two-way synchronization across CRM, accounting (QuickBooks/Stripe), and dispatch/project tools, plus human-in-the-loop approval dashboards.
  - **Ongoing Engineering Retainer ($1,250/mo):** Priority SLA response, proactive workflow monitoring, database backups, and 5 hours/month of iterative feature and automation expansion.

## Strategic pricing mechanics

- **De-Risked Entry Without Discounting:** If an owner hesitates at **$7,500**, never slash the price — down-sell cleanly to **Tier 1 ($3,500)** and offer to credit 100% of their Tier 1 fee toward Tier 2 if they upgrade within 45 days.
- **Recurring Revenue Tied to Operational Peace of Mind:** Busy owner-operators gladly pay **$650/mo** when framed as *"24/7 webhook monitoring, hosting, and ensuring your lead pipeline never silently breaks"* rather than vague "maintenance."
- **Hard Scope Ceilings:** Capping pages (3 vs. 7) and workflow counts (1 vs. 3) protects margins from scope creep while giving a natural paid upsell (+$750 per additional standalone workflow).

## Use in the call flow

- Present in the 20-min Scope & Workflow Map session (the step after the 15-min discovery call), not on call one.
- Tier 2 is the anchor to land on; Tier 1 is the down-sell with a 45-day 100% credit toward Tier 2; Tier 3 is the anchor that makes Tier 2 look obvious.
