### 1. Project Objective & Summary

Provider will design, build, and launch a conversion-focused website (up to seven pages) paired with up to three deterministic backend workflow automations for Client, followed by ongoing monitoring and support under the Care & Monitoring plan defined in Section 5.

The goal of this engagement is to improve web inquiry/booking conversion, automate routine lead routing and follow-up handoffs, and reduce manual administrative coordination for Client's team.

---

### 2. In-Scope Deliverables

#### 2.1 Custom Web Frontend (Up to 7 Pages)

* **Pages Included (up to 7 total):** Home, Services / Solutions, About / Trust & Credentials, Social Proof / Reviews or Case Studies, Quote or Booking / Intake Page, Contact Page, and Legal / Privacy Policy Page (using Client-approved legal text).
* **Design & UX:** Responsive layout engineered for mobile and desktop viewports, clear conversion hierarchy, and structured intake/booking entry points.
* **Technical Baseline:** SEO-friendly semantic headings/metadata, analytics/event tracking hookup, and performance optimization prior to launch.

#### 2.2 Backend Workflow Automations (Up to 3 Deterministic Workflows)

Provider will configure and test **up to three (3)** automated workflows connecting Client's web intake/scheduling tools to Client's existing operational stack (e.g., CRM, calendar, email/SMS, Slack, or spreadsheet/database):

1. **Workflow 1 — Speed-to-Lead & Qualification Routing:** Captures web form/intake submissions, applies deterministic routing/qualification rules, triggers instant prospect confirmation, and sends an actionable alert (email, SMS, or Slack) to Client's designated recipient.
2. **Workflow 2 — Booking & Appointment Reminders:** Connects scheduling/calendar events to automated pre-appointment reminders and rescheduling/confirmation links to reduce no-shows.
3. **Workflow 3 — Post-Completion Review / Follow-Up Loop:** Triggers a structured follow-up or review request after a completed job/appointment/inquiry milestone, with exception handling so negative or edge-case responses route to manual review.

#### 2.3 Launch, Handoff & Documentation

* Production deployment on Client's approved hosting/domain setup.
* One (1) recorded walkthrough/training session (up to 30 minutes) and concise workflow summary documentation.
* **Fourteen (14) calendar days** of post-launch bug/defect resolution on delivered in-scope items at no additional setup fee.

### 3. Revisions, Approvals & Timeline

#### 3.1 Target Timeline (3 to 4 Weeks From Kickoff)

* **Week 1:** Kickoff, asset/access collection, sitemap & workflow architecture approval.
* **Week 2:** Core UI/UX design & initial automation build on staging/test environment.
* **Week 3:** Full build assembly, end-to-end workflow testing, and Client staging review.
* **Week 4:** Final revisions, production cutover, QA verification, and handoff.

*Timelines assume timely delivery of Client inputs under Section 4. Delays in Client feedback or third-party account access shift launch dates day-for-day.*

#### 3.2 Revision Limits

* **Design & Build Revisions:** Includes **up to two (2) consolidated rounds of revisions** per major deliverable stage (1. Wireframe/Layout & Visual Direction; 2. Staging Build & Workflow QA).
* **Consolidated Feedback Rule:** Client agrees to consolidate feedback from stakeholders into a single written list per revision round within **five (5) business days** of delivery. Contradictory requests made after approval of a stage, or requests beyond two (2) rounds, are billed as Change Orders under Section 6.
* **Silent Approval Rule:** If Client does not provide feedback within **seven (7) business days** of a staging/deliverable submission, that stage is deemed approved for scheduling purposes.

### 4. Client Responsibilities & Dependencies

To keep the engagement on schedule, Client will provide:

1. **Brand & Content Assets:** Logo files, brand colors/fonts (if established), existing photos/videos, service descriptions, pricing/service-area rules, and business hours within **three (3) business days** of kickoff.
2. **System Access:** Timely admin/editor/API/webhook access to domain registrar/DNS, website host, CRM, calendar, email/SMS sender, and any third-party tools required for the three workflows.
3. **Decision Authority:** A single designated point of contact authorized to approve copy, layouts, workflow logic, and milestones on behalf of Client.

### 6. Out-of-Scope Items & Change Orders

Unless explicitly added via a signed written Change Order, the following are **out of scope** under Tier 2:

* More than **seven (7) pages**, custom web applications, authenticated member/client portals, e-commerce storefronts with complex inventory/tax logic, or custom multi-step quoting engines.
* More than **three (3) backend automation workflows**, custom ERP/legacy database migrations, custom software development from scratch, or building/managing paid ad campaigns (Google/Meta Ads management).
* Original long-form copywriting across all pages, custom photography/videography, stock asset licensing, or third-party SaaS/API/SMS/hosting subscription fees billed directly by vendors.
* Changes caused by third-party API deprecations, vendor outages, or Client/third-party edits made directly to live code or active workflows without Provider's knowledge.

**Change Order Process:** Any requested addition or material change outside this SOW will be scoped with a clear price and timeline estimate in writing before work begins. Additional standalone automations are typically scoped at **`$750` each**, and additional change-order work at **`$[XXX]/hour`** (or fixed quote).
