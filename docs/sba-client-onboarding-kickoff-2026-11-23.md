## Practical tips for running smooth onboarding

- **Use a Single Intake Form + Shared Folder:** Send one structured onboarding form alongside a shared Drive/Notion/Airtable folder right after kickoff so logins, files, and workflow answers don't get scattered across five email threads.
- **Flag SMS / A2P Early:** If an automation sends SMS confirmations or reminders, checking A2P 10DLC sender readiness on Day 1 prevents last-minute carrier delays right before launch.
- **Keep Credentials Out of Plain Email:** Have clients invite you via role/delegated permissions or share secrets through a password vault/encrypted intake link rather than pasting raw passwords into chat or email.
